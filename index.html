        Windy map Cyclone bulletins

        Wind 10 m above the ground, from Open-Meteo's forecast models on a 1° (about 110 km) grid, every 3 hours. Colours follow IMD's names for a system with that sustained wind; a coarse model often shows a cyclone's core weaker and wider than it is, so use IMD's bulletins for the official track and strength. Windy map has gusts, rain and pressure layers.

                      Rain near each spot today

                        Add your own spot

                        Move the map so the + in its middle is on the place, then add it. It is kept on this phone.

                        Spots are low-lying areas and road subways that flooded in past monsoons (2015, 2021, 2023), placed roughly. Each takes the rain today at the nearest station within 6 km: Watch from 30 mm a day or 15 mm/h, Likely flooded from 64.5 mm a day (IMD's heavy rain) or 30 mm/h. It's a rough guide from rain alone, not a flood report; check Greater Chennai Corporation's alerts before travelling.

                            AQI · next few days

                            India's National AQI (CPCB scale: Good 0–50, Satisfactory –100, Moderate –200, Poor –300, Very poor –400, Severe), worked out from the Copernicus (CAMS) pollution forecast via Open-Meteo. It is one model figure for the whole city; CPCB readings by area shows the measured values at CPCB's Chennai stations.
