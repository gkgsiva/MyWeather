              Every district, next 2 hours

                The app follows the rain on the radars over the last hour and carries it forward at the same speed and heading, up to 2 hours. Rain grows, fades and turns, so take it as a rough guide. District times are for the middle of each district. Radar: IMD Doppler Weather Radars at Chennai, Karaikal, Thiruvananthapuram, Kochi and Sriharikota; they can only be read inside the MyWeather app.

                Rain map

                By district (mean of its gauges)

                  Each spot takes the gauges within 20 km, the nearer ones counting more (inverse-distance weighting). Places with no gauge that close are left blank. Gauges that miss more than one hour in ten, or that Station health finds suspect, are left out. Rain gauges: TN-SMART (Tamil Nadu disaster management, RIMES), hourly.

                    Windy map Cyclone bulletins

                    Wind 10 m above the ground, from Open-Meteo's forecast models on a 1° (about 110 km) grid, every 3 hours. Colours follow IMD's names for a system with that sustained wind; a coarse model often shows a cyclone's core weaker and wider than it is, so use IMD's bulletins for the official track and strength. Windy map has gusts, rain and pressure layers.

                                  Station health

                                  Wunderground stations
                                    TN rain gauges
                                      TN gauges reporting, by district
                                      District Reporting Silent Silent %

                                      Checks: offline or over an hour old; impossible values (negative rain, over 150 mm in an hour, humidity 0%, dew point above the temperature); a day total that doesn't match its hours; the same rain every hour for 6 hours; and readings far from every neighbour (a gauge with 50 mm+ when the gauges within 10 km had a quarter of that, a dry gauge among wet ones, a temperature 4° off the stations within 25 km). A flag is a reason to doubt a reading, not proof: a real downpour can hit one gauge only.

                                              Wettest hours at a gauge

                                                Days and spells use TN-SMART's daily district mean (the average of the district's gauges, rain days to 8:30 AM IST), which starts on 11 Oct 2025. A spell is a run of days with 2.5 mm or more (IMD's rainy day); tap a day or spell to open it on Rain spell. Wettest hours are the biggest single hours at any gauge in the district on its 6 wettest days.

                                                              Info

                                                              MyWeather ·

                                                              Weather for Chennai and Tamil Nadu in one app. Radar, satellite, lightning, warnings, cyclones and monsoon figures come from the India Meteorological Department (IMD); rain gauges and lake history from TN-SMART (Tamil Nadu government, with RIMES); station readings from Wunderground; forecasts, heat, wind, air quality and the sea from Open-Meteo; lake levels from Chennai Metro Water; maps from OpenStreetMap; posts from the Kea Weather Blog. Nowcasts, storm movement and lightning distances are the app's own estimates.
